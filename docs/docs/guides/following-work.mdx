# My Work and subscriptions

## My Work

My Work shows items assigned directly to you or to a team you belong to, across
projects you can access. Use list, grouped, or board view, then search or filter
by project, status, and priority.

## Subscriptions

Subscribe to follow a work item without becoming its assignee. Subscribing
does not change ownership. Unsubscribe from the work item workspace or the
Subscriptions page.

subscription time. Use **Unsubscribe** there to stop following the item.

## Email notifications

When your administrator enables email, a new work-item assignment can also
arrive in your inbox. This applies to work assigned directly to you or to a team
you belong to, provided your account is active, has an email address, and has
access to the project.

Assignments are collected for about two minutes so several work items can appear
in one email. The email includes the project, work-item title, who assigned it,
any team involved, the due date when present, and a link to the item.

You do not receive assignment email for assigning work to yourself. Being
assigned directly and through a team does not produce duplicate entries in the
same email. If your assignment or project access is removed before delivery,
the item is omitted.

Subscribing does not enable email for every edit. Routine work-item edits and
subscription activity do not generate email, and marking an in-app notification
read does not cancel an assignment email.

Email is controlled by the administrator for the installation; there are no
individual email preferences yet. Administrators can follow the
[email setup guide](../reference/email-notifications).

## In-app notifications

The notification center in the navigation sidebar surfaces assignment and
work-item activity updates. Open a notification to mark it read and jump to
its project when a project link is available, or mark all notifications read
from the center.
