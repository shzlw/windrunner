# My Work and subscriptions

## My Work

My Work shows items assigned directly to you or to a team you belong to, across
projects you can access. Use list, grouped, or board view, then search or filter
by project, status, and priority.

## Subscriptions

Subscribe to follow a work item without becoming its assignee. Subscribing
does not change ownership. Unsubscribe from the work item workspace or the
Subscriptions page.
