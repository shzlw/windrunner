# Users and teams

## Users

User profiles can include a display name, title, bio, email, timezone, and
status. Administrators can create, edit, deactivate, and reset users from the
Users page.

## Teams

Teams have a name, description, owners, members, and linked projects. Team
owners review and decide join requests. Administrators manage membership and
project links. Non-superadmin users can request to join a team.

The Teams page lists member and project counts, creation dates, and your role.
Open a team to manage its description, owners, members, linked projects, and
join requests.

Teams and users are useful Ask AI context. For example, add a team when asking
which group can help with an issue, or add a user when asking about their role
and work.

Deleting a user deactivates the account and removes its memberships. Deleting a
team removes its memberships and project links.
